Book One

CONSCIOUSNESS CALLING — LIVING WITH I AM PRESENCE

Author: Raghuveera (Ra Soul)

What if the Divine Presence you have been seeking is not somewhere beyond you, but already present within? Book One explores awareness, perception, thought, emotion, identity, silence, the Heart as doorway, I AM Presence, Śiva–Śakti, and the Living Field. It invites readers to bring recognition into ordinary human living, not to abandon life or reject the mind.
