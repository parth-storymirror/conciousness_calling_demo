About the Author

Raghuveera (Ra Soul)

Raghuveera Sapineni (Ra Soul) is a technology professional and Senior Program Manager based in Bengaluru, India. Alongside professional and family responsibilities, he explores the meeting of inner awareness and everyday life.

His journey began with the question, “Who am I?” Through inquiry, devotion and practice, he came to recognize the Inner Sun—the Light of Presence within. A further question, “Where am I?”, opened a wider recognition: his life unfolding within the greater Living Field, which he reveres as the sustaining Presence of Lord Mahāviṣṇu.

Consciousness Calling emerged from listening within the Heart and learning to participate consciously in Life. His journey is rooted in devotion to Divine Mother Lalitāmbikā and the continuing Presence of Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba.

For him, the world of responsibility and the world within are inseparable dimensions of one lived experience. His writing explores how this understanding finds expression through Love, Dharma, compassion and service. He invites readers to discover through their own experience: “How does Presence live in the middle of life?”
