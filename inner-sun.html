INNER SUN — SHIVA SAKTHI FIELD

One Heart. One Consciousness. One Living Field.

Living Consciousness and Embodiment

Inner Sun invites you to explore the relationship between Divine Presence, your Soul’s experience and the life you live each day.

Within the Consciousness Calling vision, Śiva is contemplated as Silent Presence, Śakti as Divine Movement, and Mahāviṣṇu as sustaining intelligence, continuity and harmony within the Living Field. Through the Soul, Life becomes individual experience. Through the Heart, that experience becomes an opportunity for recognition, alignment and loving action.

The cycle is already whole. Our recognition of it may be incomplete. The Soul participates within this wholeness; it is not a fourth cosmic function added to the Living Triad.

The invitation is to recognise Presence within and allow that recognition to become your way of living.

These are contemplative interpretations within Consciousness Calling. They honour the diversity of Hindu philosophical traditions and are offered for personal reflection and lived exploration.

Inner Sun — Shiva Sakthi Field is the experiential Heart-centered expression of the wider Consciousness Calling vision: Śiva as silent Presence, Śakti as living expression, and Mahāviṣṇu as the Living Field in which the Soul experiences and remembers. These are contemplative functional expressions of One Consciousness, not separate or incomplete aspects of Sat–Chit–Ānanda.

One Consciousness — Three Divine Expressions

ŚIVA — Eternal Presence

Pure Being, stillness, the unchanging foundation of existence.

Sat–Chit–Ānanda, recognized as the eternal Self.

Silent Presence / Being

ŚAKTI — Divine Movement

Conscious Power, Grace, Wisdom, Love and the living expression of creation.

Sat–Chit–Ānanda, revealed through the Divine Mother's fullness.

Living Power / Expression

MAHĀVIṢṆU — Living Intelligence

Divine order, sustenance, harmony and the intelligence through which life is supported.

Sat–Chit–Ānanda, revealed through the fullness of Divine Presence.

Universal Intelligence/Sustaining Living Field / Harmony

ONE HEART. ONE CONSCIOUSNESS. ONE LIVING FIELD.

01

Śiva · Recognising Silent Presence

Behind changing thoughts, emotions and experiences, we can discover moments of quiet awareness.

Within Inner Sun, Śiva represents this Silent Presence—the stillness underlying experience. Recognising it invites us to meet life with greater steadiness, without withdrawing from our responsibilities.

An invitation: Pause for a moment. Notice your thoughts, and the awareness through which you know them.

STILLNESS → AWARENESS → PRESENCE

02

Śakti · Recognising Life in Movement

Life continually moves through feeling, creativity, relationships and action.

Śakti represents the Divine Power expressing through this movement. Our invitation is to bring awareness to how we respond, create and care, allowing our energy to find expression through Love.

An invitation: Notice where your energy is going today. What action would express care?

PRESENCE → MOVEMENT → CREATIVE EXPRESSION

03

Mahāviṣṇu · Recognising the Living Field

Our lives unfold through relationships—with people, Nature, circumstances and the wider world.

Mahāviṣṇu represents the sustaining Living Field within which these experiences unfold. This contemplation invites us to recognise interdependence and participate with greater harmony and responsibility.

An invitation: Notice how much supports your life, and how your choices affect the lives around you.

LIVING FIELD → RELATIONSHIP → HARMONY

04

Soul · Understanding Your Individual Experience

Your experiences, questions, relationships and choices are where this exploration becomes personal.

Within this framework, the Soul is the individual experiencer through whom Consciousness encounters Life. Joy, difficulty and recurring patterns can become opportunities to understand ourselves more deeply. Compassion also leaves room for pain that needs support, healing or practical change.

An invitation: What is this experience revealing about how you think, feel or respond?

EXPERIENCE → SELF OBSERVATION → INNER RECOGNITION

05

Heart · Allowing Recognition to Change Your Response

The Heart is the doorway through which inner recognition begins to influence how we live.

As we pause and listen, we may find greater compassion, discernment and connection. The mind continues to help us understand and act; the Heart helps us consider the quality and consequences of our actions.

An invitation: Before responding, pause and ask: What is truthful, caring and responsible here?

PAUSE → HEART → DISCERNMENT → RESPONSE

06

Divine Will · Participating with Love and Awareness

Alignment with Divine Will is explored through surrender, discernment and responsible participation.

We continue making choices and taking action, while becoming more willing to listen, reconsider and serve. An inner feeling can be examined patiently through compassion, Dharma, practical consequences and respect for others.

The inner relationship gradually changes:

  1. “I am the separate doer.”
  2. “I am a conscious participant in Life.”
  3. “Let Divine Love guide my actions.”

An invitation: What would Love ask me to take responsibility for today?

LISTEN → DISCERN → ALIGN → ACT → SERVE

07

Embodiment · Living Consciousness Through Everyday Life

Recognition becomes embodiment as it finds expression in ordinary moments: listening with patience, working honestly, caring for family, respecting Nature and responding with compassion.

The Soul does not have to escape the Divine Dream to recognise Divine Presence.

The transformation unfolds within Life. We continue working, loving, learning and fulfilling our responsibilities, while our relationship with those experiences deepens.

Within Consciousness Calling, 5D Consciousness describes Heart-centred living through unity, compassion, Dharma and service. Participation in Living Creation means allowing these qualities to shape what we contribute to the world.

PRESENCE → LOVE → ACTION → EMBODIMENT

Closing Invitation

Align with Divine Will. Participate in 5D Consciousness and Living Creation through Love, awareness and service.

Begin with one conscious choice in the life you are already living.
