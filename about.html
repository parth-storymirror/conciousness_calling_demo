About the Author

Raghuveera (Ra Soul)

Raghuveera, writing as Ra Soul, is the author of Consciousness Calling — Living with I AM Presence. Alongside his professional life, his personal journey of Sādhana, spiritual inquiry and inner contemplation evolved into an exploration of Consciousness, the Heart and Divine Presence within. Inspired by his personal experience of the Supreme Master Light and contemplation of Śiva, Śakti and Mahāviṣṇu, his writing invites readers to listen within, reflect, discern and allow spiritual recognition to become conscious living. The author is an instrument. The books are instruments. The invitation is for Consciousness to be recognized and lived through every Heart.
